The Sacred Kanwar Yatra

The Kanwar Yatra is the central pilgrimage associated with Shravani Mela. During the holy month of Shravan, Kanwariyas travel to Ajgaivinath Dham at Sultanganj to collect sacred Ganga water and carry it by Kanwar to Baba Baidyanath Dham in Deoghar.

The pilgrimage is traditionally undertaken on foot. Many devotees wear saffron-coloured clothing and carry decorated Kanwars containing the sacred water.

The journey represents devotion, discipline, faith, endurance and service to Lord Shiva.

कांवर यात्रा श्रावणी मेला की प्रमुख धार्मिक यात्रा है। श्रद्धालु सुल्तानगंज के अजगैवीनाथ धाम से पवित्र गंगाजल लेकर कांवर के माध्यम से बाबा बैद्यनाथ धाम, देवघर पहुँचते हैं। यह यात्रा आस्था, भक्ति, अनुशासन और समर्पण का प्रतीक है।

The Sacred Route

🌊

Ajgaivinath Dham

Sultanganj, Bihar

The sacred starting point of the Kanwar pilgrimage, located on the bank of the River Ganga.

गंगा नदी के तट पर स्थित पवित्र तीर्थस्थल और कांवर यात्रा का प्रमुख प्रारंभिक स्थान।

🚩

Kanwar Yatra

Approximately 109 kilometres

The Deoghar District Administration describes the Sultanganj-to-Babadham Kanwar pilgrimage as a 109-kilometre journey on foot.

देवघर जिला प्रशासन के अनुसार सुल्तानगंज से बाबा धाम तक कांवर यात्रा लगभग 109 किलोमीटर की पैदल यात्रा है।

🕉️

Baba Baidyanath Dham

Deoghar, Jharkhand

The sacred destination where Kanwariyas offer the holy Ganga water to Baba Baidyanath Jyotirlinga.

देवघर स्थित पवित्र धाम जहाँ कांवरिया बाबा बैद्यनाथ ज्योतिर्लिंग पर गंगाजल अर्पित करते हैं।

Distance note: The pilgrimage distance is commonly described differently in different sources. This website currently follows the 109-kilometre description published by the Deoghar District Administration for the Sultanganj–Babadham Kanwar pilgrimage.

Beginning the Pilgrimage

Pilgrims arrive at Ajgaivinath Dham and collect sacred Ganga water. The water is placed in a vessel and carried on a Kanwar.

After completing the necessary preparations and religious rituals, Kanwariyas begin their journey towards Baba Baidyanath Dham.

The route passes through areas of Bihar before continuing towards Deoghar in Jharkhand.

श्रद्धालु अजगैवीनाथ धाम पहुँचकर गंगाजल लेते हैं और उसे कांवर में रखकर बाबा बैद्यनाथ धाम की ओर पैदल यात्रा प्रारंभ करते हैं।

Important Route Facilities

Pilgrim facilities are arranged at various locations along the Kanwar route. Services may include drinking water, toilets, rest areas, medical assistance, information centres and security arrangements.

Location / Area Examples of Facilities
Ajgaivinath Dham / Sultanganj Drinking water, toilets, waiting areas, security arrangements and pilgrim facilities.
Asarganj Drinking water, toilets, resting places and public facilities.
Madhya Siddhi Ghat Bathing ghat, toilets, changing facilities, drinking water, seating and security arrangements.
Kachahari / Shivganga / Bagh Berati Drinking water, toilets, changing facilities, resting areas and waiting facilities.
Kumarganj / Bagh Berati Drinking water, toilets, changing rooms, waiting facilities, security and information services.

Rest and Night-Halt Arrangements

Temporary structures and rest shelters are arranged at various locations around the Sultanganj starting area and along important points of the pilgrimage route.

Pilgrim facilities can include hotels, dharamshalas, food services, vehicle stands and other public amenities.

Important: Facilities may change during the live Mela according to administrative arrangements, crowd conditions and the operational schedule. Pilgrims should confirm current availability locally or through official authorities before relying on a particular facility.

Medical and Emergency Support

Medical camps and health facilities are arranged at multiple locations along the Kanwariya path.

Pilgrims should seek assistance immediately in case of dehydration, exhaustion, injury or any other medical problem.

🙏 Safety First

Drink sufficient water, protect yourself from excessive heat, take adequate rest and seek medical assistance whenever necessary.

Important Advice for Kanwariyas

Carry Essentials

Keep necessary identification, medicines, mobile phone and essential personal items safely with you.

पहचान पत्र, आवश्यक दवाइयाँ, मोबाइल फोन और जरूरी व्यक्तिगत सामान सुरक्षित रखें।

Protect Your Belongings

Crowded pilgrimage areas require extra attention to phones, wallets, bags and other valuables.

भीड़भाड़ वाले क्षेत्रों में मोबाइल, बटुआ, बैग और अन्य कीमती सामान का विशेष ध्यान रखें।

Follow Authorities

Follow instructions issued by police, administration, medical teams and official help desks.

पुलिस, प्रशासन, चिकित्सा दल और आधिकारिक सहायता केंद्रों के निर्देशों का पालन करें।

Reaching Baba Baidyanath Dham

After completing the pilgrimage, Kanwariyas reach Baba Baidyanath Dham in Deoghar and offer the sacred Ganga water to Lord Shiva.

This sacred offering completes the principal religious purpose of the Kanwar Yatra.

कांवर यात्रा पूरी करने के बाद श्रद्धालु देवघर स्थित बाबा बैद्यनाथ धाम पहुँचकर भगवान शिव को पवित्र गंगाजल अर्पित करते हैं।
