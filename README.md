
🚩 Shravani Mela 2026
Official Shravani Mela 2026 Information Website

Welcome to the Shravani Mela 2026 website — an information resource dedicated to one of India's most important Shiva pilgrimages and religious fairs.

The sacred Kanwar pilgrimage connects Ajgaivinath Dham (Sultanganj), Bihar with Baba Baidyanath Dham, Deoghar, Jharkhand.

During the holy month of Shravan, Kanwariyas collect sacred Ganga water at Ajgaivinath Dham and undertake the pilgrimage on foot to Baba Baidyanath Dham.

🕉️ About Shravani Mela

Shravani Mela is associated with the holy month of Shravan and Lord Shiva.

The pilgrimage is famous for the devotion of Kanwariyas, who carry sacred Ganga water in Kanwars while travelling towards Baba Baidyanath Dham.

The traditional pilgrimage route is approximately 109 km on foot, connecting Ajgaivinath Dham with Baba Baidyanath Dham.

The pilgrimage is commonly associated with the devotional chant:

"Bol Bam"

🚩 Sacred Pilgrimage Route
Starting Point

Ajgaivinath Dham (Sultanganj), Bihar

Pilgrims collect sacred Ganga water here before beginning the Kanwar Yatra.

Destination

Baba Baidyanath Dham, Deoghar, Jharkhand

The pilgrimage concludes at the revered Baba Baidyanath Temple, where devotees offer the sacred Ganga water to Lord Shiva.

Approximate Distance

109 km on foot
