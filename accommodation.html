Accommodation & Night Halt

Rest, stay and night-halt information for pilgrims travelling from Ajgaivinath Dham (Sultanganj) towards Baba Baidyanath Dham, Deoghar.

Where Can Pilgrims Rest?

During Shravani Mela, large numbers of pilgrims travel on foot along the Kanwar route. Government authorities, local bodies, religious organisations, voluntary groups and other organisations arrange different types of temporary and permanent facilities for pilgrims.

🚩 Important:

Accommodation and resting facilities can become crowded very quickly during the peak pilgrimage period. Availability, location and operating arrangements may change. Always follow the latest instructions from the local administration and mela authorities.

Types of Accommodation

🏕️

Vishram Shalas

Temporary or permanent resting shelters where pilgrims can take a break from the long journey.

🛏️

Dharmshalas

Dharmshalas and pilgrim facilities may provide simple accommodation or resting arrangements.

⛺

Tent Facilities

Temporary tent accommodation and tent-city facilities may be arranged at selected locations during the mela.

🏫

Temporary Shelters

Schools, public buildings and other suitable premises may be used for temporary pilgrim accommodation when arranged by the authorities.

🚩

Seva Camps

Voluntary and religious organisations may operate service camps offering food, water, rest and other assistance.

🏨

Hotels & Private Stay

Visitors who prefer private accommodation can consider hotels and other lodging facilities in nearby towns.

The journey is long and physically demanding. Pilgrims should plan their rest according to their physical condition and the facilities available along the route.

Do not wait until severe exhaustion before taking a break. Use designated rest areas, service camps and official facilities whenever possible.

What Facilities May Be Available?

🚰 Drinking Water

Water arrangements are provided at various locations during the mela.

🚻 Toilets

Temporary and permanent sanitation facilities are arranged at different points.

🚿 Bathing

Bathing and changing facilities are available at selected locations.

🍛 Food & Community Kitchens

Service organisations and mela arrangements may provide food and refreshments for pilgrims.

🏥 Medical Assistance

Health camps and medical facilities are arranged at various locations.

👮 Security

Police and security arrangements operate throughout the mela area and pilgrimage route.

Advice Before Choosing a Night Halt

  • Prefer official or clearly identified pilgrim facilities.
  • Ask the local mela help desk about current availability.
  • Do not block the Kanwar route while resting.
  • Keep your belongings with you.
  • Keep children and elderly family members close.
  • Do not accept suspicious accommodation offers from strangers.
  • Keep your mobile phone charged.
  • Drink sufficient water and take regular rest.
  • If you become seriously ill, seek medical assistance immediately.

For Families, Elderly Pilgrims and Children

Families travelling with children or elderly pilgrims should plan rest more carefully. The pace of the journey should be appropriate to the physical condition of every member of the group.

❤️ A simple rule:

Faith requires devotion, but a long pilgrimage also requires care. Rest when necessary, stay hydrated and seek medical help whenever required.

Hotels and Private Accommodation

Pilgrims and visitors who want private accommodation can consider hotels, guest houses and other lodging facilities in and around Sultanganj, along the wider route and near Deoghar.

During the peak mela period, rooms may become difficult to find. Visitors should verify the current price, location, availability and booking conditions directly before making travel arrangements.

⚠️ Avoid unverified claims:

This website will not publish accommodation prices, telephone numbers or availability unless they can be verified from a reliable and current source.

ठहरने और रात्रि पड़ाव की व्यवस्था

अजगैवीनाथ धाम (सुल्तानगंज) से बाबा बैद्यनाथ धाम, देवघर की ओर जाने वाले श्रद्धालुओं के लिए विश्राम, ठहरने और रात्रि पड़ाव की जानकारी।

श्रद्धालु कहां विश्राम कर सकते हैं?

श्रावणी मेले के दौरान बड़ी संख्या में श्रद्धालु कांवर मार्ग पर पैदल यात्रा करते हैं। सरकारी प्रशासन, स्थानीय निकाय, धार्मिक संस्थाएं, स्वयंसेवी समूह और अन्य संगठन श्रद्धालुओं के लिए विभिन्न प्रकार की अस्थायी और स्थायी सुविधाएं उपलब्ध कराते हैं।

🚩 महत्वपूर्ण:

मेले के चरम समय में ठहरने और विश्राम की सुविधाएं बहुत जल्दी भर सकती हैं। उपलब्धता, स्थान और संचालन व्यवस्था बदल सकती है। हमेशा जिला प्रशासन और मेला प्रशासन के नवीनतम निर्देशों का पालन करें।
