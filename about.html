About Shravani Mela

A sacred journey of faith from Ajgaivinath Dham, Sultanganj, to Baba Baidyanath Dham, Deoghar.

The World's Longest Religious Fair

Shravani Mela is one of the most significant religious pilgrimages associated with Lord Shiva. During the holy month of Shravan, vast numbers of devotees undertake the sacred Kanwar pilgrimage from Sultanganj in Bihar to Baba Baidyanath Dham in Deoghar, Jharkhand.

The Deoghar District Administration describes Shravani Mela as the world's longest religious fair. The pilgrimage is marked by devotion, discipline, service and the spirit of “Bol Bam”.

🚩 The Sacred Route

The Kanwar pilgrimage is described by the Deoghar District Administration as approximately 109 km on foot, connecting the sacred Ganga at Sultanganj with Baba Baidyanath Dham at Deoghar.

From Ajgaivinath Dham to Baba Baidyanath Dham

The pilgrimage begins at Ajgaivinath Dham in Sultanganj, where Kanwariyas collect sacred water from the Ganga. Carrying the water in their Kanwar, pilgrims begin their journey towards Baba Baidyanath Dham.

The Kanwariyas

The pilgrims who undertake this journey are commonly known as Kanwariyas. Many travel wearing saffron clothing and carry a Kanwar bearing the sacred Ganga water.

The journey is undertaken on foot and is an expression of devotion and dedication to Lord Shiva. Along the route, pilgrims encounter resting places, camps, medical facilities, community kitchens, volunteers and large numbers of fellow devotees.

The Sacred Offering

After completing the pilgrimage, Kanwariyas reach Deoghar and proceed to Baba Baidyanath Dham. Devotees traditionally offer the sacred Ganga water they have carried from Sultanganj to Lord Shiva at the temple.

The journey therefore connects two sacred places through a continuous tradition of faith: the holy Ganga at Ajgaivinath Dham and Baba Baidyanath Dham at Deoghar.
